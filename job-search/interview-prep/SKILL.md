# Interview prep

Getting an interview is the moment to go deeper than the job-fit read. The aim is that she walks
in knowing the company better than most candidates, with stories and numbers ready.

Read `../job-fit/references/private/candidate-profile.md` and `jobs/<company>/posting.md`.

## 1. Update the tracker

Set the entry's status to `interviewing` and `next` to the interview step (ArtifactData
`update`, collection `applications`, pin `if_version`).

## 2. Research the company

Save findings to `jobs/<company>/company-research.md`.

- Basics: founded, founders and leaders, what they sell and to whom, size, offices, model.
- People: team page headcount by function; whether marketing staff exist; LinkedIn needs a login,
  so skip it rather than ask.
- News: press releases and outside coverage, newest first.
- Sentiment: client reviews (Google, Trustpilot, G2, Birdeye) and employee reviews (Glassdoor,
  Indeed). Say plainly when none exist.
- Marketing audit when the role is marketing: website, blog cadence and last post date, social
  follower counts and last post, YouTube, reviews shown or missing, local pages, obvious gaps.
  Many sites load content with JavaScript, so open blog and team pages in the built-in browser
  (wait a few seconds, click "View more") before concluding a section is empty. If she sees
  something different, trust her and recheck.
- Turn gaps into 5 to 7 quick wins she could pitch.

## 3. Prep the conversation

- Her three ready stories: HealthSnap funnel rebuild (214%), HealthSnap ABM, and the one most
  relevant to this role (contractor-led marketing, Marketo launches, AI builds).
- Answer pattern: answer, give one result, tie it to their company without waiting to be asked.
- Ask early what matters most in this hire.
- "How do you approach challenges": name the problem and the number it affects, look at the data,
  talk to the people closest to it, run a small test, share what you learn, ask for help early.
- Practice for a flat, hard-to-read interviewer.
- 5 to 6 questions to ask: reporting line, why the seat is open, success in 90 days, budget and
  team or contractors, current lead volume and conversion, how pay or bonus works.
- Offer a 30-60-90 day outline if the conversation stalls.

## 4. Negotiation (when pay comes up)

Save to `jobs/<company>/negotiation.md`:
- Three numbers she sets first: walk-away, target, ask.
- Scripts for profit sharing or bonus questions, salary expectations, and pushback on a posted cap.
- After an offer: get it in writing, ask for 24 to 48 hours, counter once with a number and reasons,
  then trade for other items if base won't move.
- Other levers: title, bonus formula in writing, sign-on bonus (typically 5% to 10% of base),
  performance bonus, six-month raise review, permission to keep her studio, benefits, flexible
  hours, home office and tools budget, clear marketing budget.
- Leverage: mention other processes only if they're real at the time.

## 5. Optional extras

- Unlisted plan page on simonnemarie.com (noindex, not in sitemap or nav), built from public
  research only: plan-on-a-page grid, a few signature ideas with impact and effort, "From my
  experience" lines. Draft in chat, get her approval, then publish with the update-site skill.
- Same-day thank-you email draft that references one specific thing from the conversation.

## Reply

Lead with the most useful findings in short sections, end with the file paths and an offer for
the next prep step. Cite sources.
