# Kickoff Brief review

A Kickoff Brief is the intake form clients fill out at /kickoff. The review turns form answers
into a clear picture of the client and a short list of questions for the kickoff call. She said
the interpretation section is the part she loves most, so it goes in every review.

## 1. Find the submission

Search Gmail for the HubSpot notification: `new submission on the HubSpot Form "Kickoff Brief"`.
The email contains the full brief. The HubSpot MCP connector points at a different portal (not
the studio one) and has no Kickoff Brief property, so don't look there. Check the project
memory's connector note if anything changes.

## 2. Review

Write it in this order:

1. **Snapshot:** who they are, what they sell, who they serve, goals, services requested,
   budget, timeline.
2. **Brief vs. live site:** a table comparing what the brief says with what their current site
   shows (pages, offer, voice, visuals, tracking, calls to action).
3. **Settle these with them:** every place where answers pull against each other or against what
   they actually do, each with what to show or ask. Word them as things to settle together,
   never as mistakes. Examples of the pattern: a "playful" voice picked alongside formal serif
   fonts (show two directions); a "Shop" page requested by a business that doesn't sell online;
   a certification whose exact name needs confirming before it goes on the site.
4. **Recommended package:** match the scope to the studio's engagement models and pricing ("The
   Investment"; see the pricing memory), with a short why.
5. **Kickoff call agenda:** 5 to 7 questions drawn from section 3.

Use only what the client provided or what's on their site. Confirm any claim (awards,
certifications, numbers) before it goes into site copy.

## 3. Track it

Add or update the prospect in the Kickoff Brief Tracker artifact (ArtifactData, url in the
project memory's tracker note, collection `prospects`, doc id = company slug). Stages: received,
reviewed, call, proposal, won, hold, lost. Fields include name, company, website, source, stage,
received, services, budget, value, monthly, deadline, next, nextDate, link, notes, updated. Pin
`if_version` on updates.

## Privacy

Client details stay private: no client names in public copy, commits, or posts without her OK.
Her privacy page promises client work isn't used for AI training, so keep that true.
