# Content in Simonne's voice

She reviews every piece of copy before it goes live. Her first blog post went out without a
review step and needed fixes afterward, so the habit now is: draft in chat, wait for her
approval, then publish.

## Voice

- Plain, specific sentences she'd say out loud. Warm and direct.
- No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or a colon.
- No "it wasn't X, it was Y" or "not this, but that" contrasts. State the point directly.
- Avoid AI-sounding patterns she has flagged:
  - openers that comment on the topic ("The core of this is...")
  - vague praise ("systems they can rely on", "where it makes the biggest difference")
  - neat summary lines that restate the point
  - generalized-wisdom tails ("and in my experience...")
  - trailing clauses praising adoption ("reporting leadership runs on")
  - setup-and-tag sentences ("X is a marketing ops problem, and it's one I solve")
  - phrasing real experience as goals ("I want to go further with...")
  - "Whether you're...", "elevate", stacked adjectives
- Paraphrase source material instead of lifting its signature phrases.

## Facts

- Use only facts she has confirmed. For case studies, the HealthSnap and PrimaSun portfolio decks
  are the source of truth (see the reference memory). Only HealthSnap and Harness have case
  studies; VendGogh and PrimaSun case studies are on hold.
- VendGogh's AI pipeline was built by an engineering partner; she set the strategy.
- Never name a private client in public copy without her OK.

## Publishing rules

- Paste the full draft in chat and wait for her approval or edits.
- After approval, publish with the project's own skill (update-site for simonnemarie.com or the
  studio site, the shop blog PR flow for minimagicmakersco.com).
- Never put "draft" in titles, file names, slugs, or copy of anything publishable.
- No Claude attribution anywhere public: no Co-Authored-By trailers, no "Generated with" lines.
